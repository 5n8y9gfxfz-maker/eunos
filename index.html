Eunos

A web browser for Mac and iPhone that is as light as we can make it and as private as a browser can be. Built on WebKit, the engine behind Safari.

Mac preview, macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon. We email you the download link by hand. Your address is used for that and nothing else.

Private by default

  • No telemetry, no analytics, no crash reports. Eunos never phones home.
  • Trackers and fingerprinting blocked on every page; each tab shows how many it stopped.
  • Non-tracking search (DuckDuckGo) by default.
  • Profiles keep cookies and history apart; private tabs never touch disk and lock with Touch ID or Face ID.
  • Optional sync goes only to your own iCloud, end-to-end encrypted.

Light

  • Idle tabs are offloaded from memory and wake when you return.
  • No background helpers, no polling, a small app.

Passkeys

Passkey sign-in through iCloud Keychain, the same way Safari does it, is coming in a later preview.

Eunos is based on Search by Office Commun (MIT).
